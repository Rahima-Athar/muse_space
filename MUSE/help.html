Frequently Asked Questions

How do I create a MUSE account?

Click the Sign Up button in the navbar, then enter your name, username, email, and password. Once registered, you can personalise your profile and start sharing work.

How do I publish a project?

Open the Create page from the navbar, add a title, cover image, description, and category, then publish. Your project will appear on your portfolio and in the community feed.

Can I edit or delete my projects?

Yes. Open your project from your portfolio and use the edit or delete options. You remain in control of everything you publish.

How do bookmarks work?

Click the bookmark icon on any project to save it. All saved work is collected in your Saved Inspirations page for easy reference.

What are weekly challenges?

Challenges are themed creative prompts released by the studio. Submit your interpretation and get a chance to be featured in the community showcase.

How is my data handled?

We respect your privacy. Read our Privacy Policy to learn what information we collect and how it is used.

Still need help?

If you cannot find an answer here, reach out to our team through the community feed or the contact details shared on your profile page. We are always happy to help.
