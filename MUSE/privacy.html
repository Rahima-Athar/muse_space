Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters. This policy explains what we collect, how we use it, and the control you have over your information.

Last updated: November 2026

1. Introduction

MUSE is a creative community where artists and designers share their work. This Privacy Policy describes how we handle information when you use our website, create an account, or interact with other creators.

2. Information We Collect

  • Account details such as your name, username, email address, and profile picture.
  • Content you share including projects, descriptions, images, comments, and bookmarks.
  • Usage information such as pages visited and interactions, used to improve the experience.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to operate your account, display your portfolio, deliver activity updates, respond to support requests, and keep the community safe. We do not sell your personal information.

4. Cookies & Local Storage

MUSE uses browser storage to remember your session and preferences. You can clear this data at any time through your browser settings, though some features may stop working as expected.

5. Sharing Your Content

Projects and profile information you mark as public are visible to other visitors. You are always in control of what you publish and can edit or remove your content from your account.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your information. However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Your Rights

You may access, update, or delete your account information at any time from your settings. If you need help exercising these rights, reach out through our Help Center.

8. Children's Privacy

MUSE is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with information, please contact us.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the date at the top of this page. Continued use of MUSE means you accept the updated policy.

10. Contact Us

Questions about this policy? Visit the Help Center and we will be happy to assist.
